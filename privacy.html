Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Short version: JPMAMPB does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. All of your trade entries, portfolio holdings, notes, and photos are stored locally on your device. We have no account system, no analytics tracking, and no remote backend for your journal.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

JPMAMPB is designed to be a fully offline-first application. We do not collect:

  • Personal identifying information (name, email, phone, address)
  • Financial account credentials or brokerage logins
  • Trade history, holdings, or portfolio data
  • Photos or notes you attach to trade entries
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or advertising profiles
  • Location data, contacts, or calendar information
  • Crash logs tied to your identity

Because we have no remote backend for journal content, there is nothing to collect, transmit, or breach. Your journal is a file on your iPhone.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All data you create in JPMAMPB is stored locally on your device using standard iOS application storage. This includes:

  • Trade entries (buy, sell, dividend, split)
  • Portfolio holdings, watchlists, and price alerts
  • Tags, notes, and free-form text
  • Photos and receipt images you attach to entries
  • App preferences (theme, default currency, display options)

This data is governed entirely by you. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app, or export it at any time via the in-app CSV / JSON export tools.

3. Information You Choose to Share

JPMAMPB only shares information when you explicitly take an action to share it:

  • Exports: When you tap Export, JPMAMPB generates a CSV or JSON file containing the data you selected. The file is produced locally and is only shared if you choose to share it through the iOS share sheet.
  • Support emails: If you contact us at datltan1991@icloud.com, we receive whatever you include in your message. Do not include sensitive personal or financial data you do not want shared with us.

We never access your journal contents, photos, or device data outside of what you voluntarily send us.

4. Third-Party Services

JPMAMPB does not embed third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or social SDKs. There are no third parties receiving data from your use of the app.

Market price data displayed in the app is fetched from public sources over standard HTTPS connections. These requests do not include personal identifiers beyond what your device and network transmit by default to any website, and they are not associated with a JPMAMPB user account.

5. Permissions Requested by the App

iOS requires apps to declare the reason for any permission they request. JPMAMPB requests the following:

  • Camera — so you can attach receipts, broker statements, and notes to your trade entries. Used only when you tap the camera button inside an entry.
  • Photo Library (read) — so you can attach existing screenshots and documents to trades. Used only when you tap the photo picker.
  • Photo Library (write) — so you can save exported portfolio reports and chart snapshots. Used only when you tap Save to Photos.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. JPMAMPB will continue to function; features that depend on a revoked permission will be unavailable until you re-enable it.

6. Children's Privacy

JPMAMPB is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Because we collect no information at all, this is structurally enforced.

7. Data Security

Because your data lives on your device, its security is governed by iOS device-level protections: passcode, Face ID / Touch ID, and full-device encryption at rest. We recommend enabling a device passcode and keeping your iOS version up to date.

JPMAMPB does not transmit your journal data over the internet.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete on our side. On your device you can:

  • Export your complete trade history or holdings snapshot as CSV or JSON at any time
  • Delete any individual entry from within the app
  • Delete everything by uninstalling the app

9. International Users

JPMAMPB is offered globally. Because we do not transfer personal data to any server, cross-border data protection frameworks (including GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and LGPD) are satisfied by design: there is no personal data leaving your device for us to process.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this Privacy Policy in a material way, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and surface a notice in the app. Because we collect no data, no change to this policy can retroactively affect data we never held.
