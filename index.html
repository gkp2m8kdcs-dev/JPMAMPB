What is JPMAMPB?

JPMAMPB is a portfolio journal you actually open every day — built around the conviction that disciplined record-keeping is the highest-leverage habit in retail trading. It combines a fully featured trade journal with institutional-grade market visualization, portfolio analytics, and an in-app investing curriculum. Everything lives on your device: no sign-in, no cloud sync, no tracking.

Offline-first No account required No tracking Your data, your device

Trade Journal

Capture every buy, sell, dividend, and split with full context — quantity, price, fees, venue, notes, color-coded tags, and photo receipts. Your complete trading history in one searchable, exportable record.

  • Buys, sells, dividends, and stock splits
  • Fees, venue, and currency per entry
  • Color-coded tags and free-form notes
  • Photo receipts from camera or photo library
  • CSV and JSON export whenever you need a backup

Market Visualization

Institutional-grade charts rendered natively in SwiftUI — no third-party chart dependencies. Inspect any ticker across global exchanges with the depth of detail professional platforms rely on.

Candlestick · Line · Mountain

Three chart modes, drawn natively on-device for crisp rendering and instant interaction.

Moving Averages

MA 7, MA 25, and MA 99 overlays for trend context at a glance.

Volume Bars

Volume confirmation baked into every chart view.

Portfolio Analytics

Manage multiple portfolios side-by-side and analyze real-time allocation drift across sectors and exchanges. Simulate strategies and rebalance with clear buy/sell recommendations.

  • Multi-portfolio tracking with side-by-side comparison
  • Real-time allocation drift by sector and exchange
  • Dollar-cost averaging simulator
  • Bear / bull price scenario modeling
  • Target-weight rebalancing with explicit recommendations

Learn

A built-in curriculum for independent investors who want to internalize the math and psychology behind every position.

  • Eight evidence-based investing articles
  • Five structured lessons from beginner to advanced — watchlists, financial statements, risk management, tax efficiency, and options hedging
  • Twenty-term glossary for quick reference

Privacy by Design

JPMAMPB does not collect personal data. There is no account, no analytics SDK, and no remote backend for your journal. Your trades, portfolios, and notes stay on your device unless you choose to export them.
